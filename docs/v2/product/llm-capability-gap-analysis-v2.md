# LLM Capability Gap Analysis v2

## LLM capabilities

Current LLMs can already provide:

1. User intent understanding
- Understand couple travel, first visit, budget, pace and emotional goals.

2. Candidate plan generation
- Generate daily routes, attractions, food and accommodation suggestions.

3. Experience design
- Combine culture, photography, food and relaxation.

## Product gaps

### Destination Intelligence

LLM knows many places but does not reliably know priority.

Example:
For first-time Beijing visitors:
Great Wall and Forbidden City may have higher value than niche locations.

### Reality Layer

Need verification for:
- tickets
- opening hours
- reservations
- transport
- prices

### Decision Making

Need to decide:
- what to keep
- what to remove
- what is most valuable

## Conclusion

The product should augment LLM decision making instead of only adding search.
